Home / Specialities / Fresh fruit

Fresh fruit exports, kept cold and on time

Perishable cargo has no slack. We book the reefer space, get the settings and paperwork right first time, and follow the box through to sailing.

What we handle for fruit exporters

With a focus on freshness and quality, we offer specialised logistics for fresh fruit, making sure it's delivered on time and preserved from source to destination.

Reefer bookings

20ft and 40ft reefer space booked with the right carrier for the season, with set temperature, ventilation and humidity on the booking, and cold-treatment requirements flagged for the markets that need them.

Documents we check before sailing

  • PPECB export certificate· inspection and certification for perishable exports
  • Phytosanitary certificate· required by the importing country
  • Certificate of origin· for preferential duty in the destination market
  • Shipping instructions and VGM· submitted before the carrier's cut-off
  • Bill of lading or sea waybill· validated against your sales contract

Home / Specialities / Wine & liquid bulk

Wine and liquid bulk, delivered as it left

Our experience in wine and liquid bulk logistics means safe, specialised handling that keeps the quality and integrity of your liquids intact across the supply chain.

Flexitank or ISO tank?

Most bulk wine moves in a flexitank: a single-use bag inside a standard 20ft container, holding up to about 24,000 litres. ISO tank containers are reusable steel tanks, better suited to repeat flows and to liquids that need heating or that are classed as hazardous.

What we arrange

  • Equipment· flexitank supply and fitting, or ISO tank booking
  • Carrier approval· not every line accepts flexitanks on every route
  • Documents· export certificates, certificate of origin, B/L
  • Insurance· cover for the full value of the cargo

Home / Specialities / Dangerous goods

Dangerous goods by air and sea, declared properly

We're equipped to move dangerous goods by both air and sea, with strict adherence to safety regulations and procedures so every shipment is secure and compliant.

What we need from you

A dangerous goods booking is only accepted if the details are complete. Send us these with your enquiry and we can check carrier acceptance straight away.

  • UN number and proper shipping name· from the safety data sheet
  • Class and packing group· for example Class 3, PG II
  • Safety data sheet (SDS)· current version
  • Packaging and quantities· inner and outer packaging, net quantity per package

The rules we work to

Sea: the IMDG Code, with the DG declaration and container packing certificate. Air: the IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations, with the shipper's declaration for dangerous goods.

Our project cargo service caters for the unique needs of large-scale and complex shipments, with customised solutions for oversized and heavy items.

Cargo that doesn't fit in a box

Machinery, vehicles, aircraft and structures that are too long, wide, tall or heavy for a standard container go on flat racks or open tops, or as breakbulk on the ship's deck.
