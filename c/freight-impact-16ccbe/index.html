Shipping a car?

Container or RORO

Two ways to ship a vehicle. Tell us the route and we'll confirm which one the port supports.

Container

Most protected
  • Enclosed 20 or 40-foot container
  • Secured with cables and straps
  • Household goods can travel with the vehicle
  • Pickup and delivery anywhere in South Africa

RORO

Usually cheapest
  • Vehicle is driven onto the ship's lower deck
  • Fastest to load and offload
  • Vehicle only, nothing packed inside
  • Depends on the port, so check availability first
